Platform

Everything your institution needs, beautifully unified.

One installable platform covering the full academic lifecycle — built with dedicated, permission-checked dashboards for every role.

Role-based dashboards

Separate administrator, teacher and student journeys with secure navigation, dedicated layouts and permission validation on every screen.

Offline-first PWA

Installable to any device with a service worker that caches the app shell, so classrooms stay productive even on unreliable networks.

Academic records

Attendance & timetable

How it works

From sign-in to insight in three steps.

Every role gets a focused, guided path — no clutter, no confusion, no unnecessary access.

01

Sign in with your role

Students and teachers sign in securely with Google; administrators use a protected credential login — never the reverse.

02

Land on your dashboard

Each role opens straight into a purpose-built dashboard with only the navigation and tools relevant to them.

03

Work, even offline

Changes sync automatically when a connection is available, so nothing is lost between the classroom and the cloud.
